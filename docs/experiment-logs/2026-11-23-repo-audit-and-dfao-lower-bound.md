## Finding 1 — `C*(N)` cannot see automata past ~4 states (lead-rerun)

`experiments/algebraic_relation.py --complexity` searches the dense power
basis `f^0..f^D` with `D <= 8`. A `k`-state automatic sequence is algebraic of
degree up to about `2^k`, and its Christol relation lives in the Frobenius
basis `f^(2^i)`. So for `k >= ~5` no relation exists inside the searched grid
and the only fit is the forced one at `C ~ N`.

Measured with the repo's own `min_ext_for_degree` (N = 128/256/512/1024):

| Sequence | min states | `C*/N` |
|---|---|---|
| Thue-Morse | 2 | 12 flat (0.012 at N=1024) |
| Rudin-Shapiro, paperfolding | 4 | 18 flat |
| random 4-state DFAO | 4 | 25 flat |
| random 8-state table | 7 | 0.984, 0.996, 0.996, 0.998 |
| random 16-state table | 14 | 1.000, 0.996, 0.996, 0.998 |
| **Rule 30 centre (ledger)** | - | 0.984, 0.996, 0.996, 0.996 |

A 7-state automatic sequence is indistinguishable from Rule 30 and from noise.
The row's "maximal algebraic complexity" and "the `s*(n)` question reached at
N=1024" were readings of the `D <= 8` cap. The 85x Thue-Morse separation is
real but is a 2-state control. Row scoped down in the ledger.

## Finding 2 — the annihilator negative is generic (lead-reviewed, agent-run)

Ledger row "No GF(2) annihilator of degree <= 3 over windows to 64 bits":
the class has `log2 M = D <= 43,745` against 7.5M-10M distinct windows, each
one constraint. By the repo's own Admission Rule that is non-discriminating,
and a seeded uniform random 200k stream run through the same `search()` gives
the identical verdict - full rank at (w,d) = (24,3) 2325/2325, (32,2) 529/529,
(64,2) 2081/2081. The Reed-Muller gate only excludes *forced* negatives; it
does not make a negative discriminating, and the 2026-09-03 log's "a genuine
constraint on Rule 30 rather than a restatement of its randomness" conflated
the two. The exclusion itself is true and exact (independent elimination on a
200k prefix agrees; `p(20) = 1,048,480` and 9,999,937 distinct 64-windows
recomputed). Regraded to a bounded `exact-exclusion`; "closes the algebraic
shortcut route" removed.

## New instrument — exact Myhill-Nerode lower bound on `s*(n)`

The certified `s*(n)` curve stopped at n=64 (a 2.26 GB DRAT proof) with
"n=72+ needs a better encoding". A *lower* bound needs no SAT. For
`i = 2^J m + r`, `m >= 1`, the digit string is `digits(m)` followed by `r`
padded to `J` digits, so (MSD) the vector `(c[2^j m + r])_{j<=J, r<2^j}` is a
function of the state reached on `digits(m)`, and (LSD) the row
`(c[2^J m + r])_m` is a function of the state reached on `r`. Distinct vectors
over fully defined prefix positions are pairwise-incompatible states: a clique
no don't-care can merge. `experiments/dfao_state_lower_bound.py`.

Command: `python experiments/dfao_state_lower_bound.py --out data/prize/2026-11-23-dfao-state-lower-bound.json` (70 s CPU).
Verifier: `python experiments/dfao_state_lower_bound.py --verify data/prize/2026-11-23-dfao-state-lower-bound.json`.

Controls and cross-checks:

- Soundness: 1200 sequences generated by random 1-12 state DFAOs, bound never
  exceeds the generator (0 violations, 225 tight). Unit test compares against
  an exhaustive brute-force exact minimum sharing no code.
- Thue-Morse: exactly 2 in both directions at n=65536.
- **Agreement with SAT:** the bound gives **13 at n=56 and 15 at n=64 (MSD)** -
  exactly the SAT/DRAT-certified `s*`. Two unrelated methods, same numbers; the
  n=64, s=14 refutation that cost a 2.26 GB proof has a 15-element
  distinguishing set.

| n | MSD `s* >=` | random band (7 seeds) | LSD `s* >=` | random band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 64 | 15 | 12..15 | 8 | 7..8 |
| 1,024 | 127 | 125..127 | 76 | 74..83 |
| 65,536 | 7,274 | 7,221..7,277 | 3,858 | 3,823..3,862 |
| 1,000,000 | 62,499 | 62,498..62,499 | 32,767 | 32,765..32,768 |
| 10,000,000 | **624,927** | 624,897..624,918 | **262,144** | 262,142..262,144 |

Full 18-length table in the artifact. Centre is inside the band in 33 of 36
cells and above it in 3 (LSD n=32; MSD n=128 and n=10M, by 1, 1 and 9 states),
never below. With a 7-seed band each cell has a 1/8 chance of exceeding the
maximum by chance, so 3 of 36 is below the expected 4.5: not an anomaly.

## Counting bound (CLAUDE.md rule 1)

`purpose: exact-exclusion`. At the excluded size `s = 624,926`,
`log2 M = 2s*log2(s) + s ~ 2.47e7 >= n = 1e7` for labelled tables, so the union
bound does **not** show vacuity - but the random control does: a coin gets the
same bound. The honest reading is the row-70 one extended 150,000x in `n`:
the centre column needs as many DFAO states as a random string, at every
length to 10M, where a genuinely automatic sequence needs O(1). It is a lower
bound on `s*`, not `s*` itself, and base 2 only.
